# Photos — Long Room Pilates (Carriage)

One room, one light, plus mat work in white space:

- **The studio series** — Ahmet Kurt (ids 25596665–25602467): one white room with arched mirrors and
  honey-wood apparatus — a reformer, a tower and a box — two clients and an instructor guiding one of
  them. It carries the first screen, one to one, four sessions, the equipment, the room and the
  booking band. Frames are cropped to **one client and one apparatus**; wide views of the room that
  show several reformers are not used, so no photograph suggests a group class.
- **Mat work** — Polina Tankilevitch (6516xxx on a white sweep, 6739xxx in window light): one woman on
  a mat in an empty white room. It carries the mat and mobility sessions, the room and first session.
